# Build log

The log has one entry for each day of work, with the newest day last. It leaves out the hull, the sensors and the tactics, which stay private at least until the competition on 13 December 2026.

## Thu 19 Nov 2026, day 1

The project starts in the evening from an empty folder. The first thing written is a simulator of the pool and of a match between two boats, so that ideas can be tried before anything is bought. The kickoff video calls this phase 0, gamification.

- The simulator gets its tests, a replay view and a tournament mode that records every match.
- Research notes are written on the drive, the electronics, the hull material and the printing.
- The work queue and the decision queue are taken into use.

## Fri 20 Nov 2026, day 2

The day goes to the build plan and to finding out how far it is from reality.

- The three agent swarms exchange their first messages through the work queue.
- The build plan goes through several drafts. A cold reader finds 119 faults in one of them, and a paper build finds 29 gaps in the shopping list and the instructions.
- The plan is moved onto data files, and a script now checks that its sums, dates and hours agree with the data.
- The hour count comes out above the hours the builder has, so the plan names what is dropped first.
- The shopping list becomes a phone page that is grouped by shop and has a tick box on every row.
- The hull model gets its open points solved, and the model stays private.
- The 30-second kickoff video is made.

Nothing has been bought yet. The first purchases are due on Mon 23 Nov 2026.
